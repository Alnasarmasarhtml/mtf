how the pad pays
01

a coin launches on a bonding curve priced in meme10, or in the mtf you picked. people buy it with the pair, not with sol.

02

every trade pays 1%, in the pair. the program splits it on the spot: 45% to the lock, 40% to the creator, 15% to mtf.

03

the 45% is already the pair, so nothing gets swapped. it goes into a vault nobody can open. less of the pair in free float, more of it locked. that's the flywheel.
